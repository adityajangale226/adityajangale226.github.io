The Heart

CA-8 Calibre

The CA-8 represents four decades of accumulated horological expertise, condensed into 847 parts spanning a movement diameter of 32mm. Assembled by a single master watchmaker — a tradition maintained since 1924 — each movement passes 168 quality checks before installation.
