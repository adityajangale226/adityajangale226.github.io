Engineered to Perfection

Why Apex Stands Alone

Every component is obsessively crafted, from the 847-part movement to the sapphire crystal that crowns it.

Grade-5 Titanium Case

Three times stronger than steel, yet 45% lighter. Our aerospace-grade titanium case withstands extreme pressures while providing unmatched comfort on the wrist.

In-House Movement

847 meticulously hand-assembled components. Our proprietary CA-8 calibre beats at 36,000 vph delivering precision to ±2 seconds per day — COSC certified.

Sapphire Crystal

Anti-reflective double-dome sapphire crystal rated 9 on the Mohs hardness scale. Virtually scratch-proof with 99.9% light transmission for perfect dial visibility.

500m Water Resistance

Helium escape valve and triple-sealed crown ensure performance to 500 metres depth. Built for professional diving while maintaining couture elegance.

Superluminova™ Indices

Grade A1 Superluminova coating on all hour markers and hands guarantees 8+ hours of full luminescence in complete darkness — essential for professional use.

Lifetime Certification

Each Apex ships with a numbered Certificate of Authenticity, COSC timing certificate, and lifetime craftsmanship warranty — your investment, protected forever.

42mm Diameter

Perfectly proportioned at 42mm diameter and 12.4mm thickness. The brushed and polished finishing involves 23 distinct surface treatments over 6 days.

Red Rubber Strap

Hypoallergenic vulcanized natural rubber strap with red lacquered titanium deployant clasp. Crafted to endure extreme conditions from arctic cold to equatorial heat.

The Craft

Materials Without Compromise

Grade-5 Titanium Alloy
Ti-6Al-4V aerospace-grade titanium. Density 4.43 g/cm³. Biocompatible, anti-magnetic, corrosion resistant to ISO 12188 standards.
Vulcanized Red Rubber
Natural rubber vulcanized at 160°C. Maintains flexibility from −40°C to +100°C. Textured for grip, hypoallergenic, salt-water resistant.
Anti-Reflective Sapphire
Synthetic corundum (Al₂O₃). Mohs hardness 9. Double-dome profile with multilayer AR coating on both surfaces. 99.9% light transmission.
Hand-Decorated Movement
Côtes de Genève finishing on bridges, perlage on mainplate, bevelled & polished edges on every single wheel. 6 days of hand decoration per movement.

A Century of Excellence

Our Legacy

1924
Founded in Geneva
Master horologist Jean-Pierre Apex establishes a workshop on Rue du Rhône, Geneva, with a singular vision: to make time feel eternal.
1961
First In-House Calibre
Launch of the CA-1 movement — the world's first chronograph calibre with a micro-rotor and column wheel, winning the Grand Prix de Genève.
1987
Titanium Pioneer
Chronos Apex becomes the first manufacture to use aerospace-grade titanium in watchmaking, a decade before it became industry standard.
2024
The Apex Chronograph
100 years of mastery culminates in our greatest achievement — 50 pieces, each requiring 1,200 hours of hand work, representing the absolute pinnacle of the art.
